### Pancreas and spleen embryology, retroperitoneal structures

1. A 2-day-old with Down syndrome has bilious vomiting. Imaging shows a ring of pancreatic tissue encircling the second part of the duodenum. Abnormal migration of which structure causes this?
   - **A.** Dorsal mesogastrium — Wrong. Forms the spleen's mesentery.
   - **B.** Vitelline duct — Wrong. Meckel diverticulum.
   - **C.** Ventral pancreatic bud **(Correct)** — Correct. Abnormal rotation of the ventral bud forms an annular pancreas around the 2nd part of the duodenum.
   - **D.** Dorsal pancreatic bud — Wrong. The ventral bud is responsible.
   - **E.** Hepatic diverticulum — Wrong. Forms the liver and biliary tree.
   - Page: 367

2. Which part of the pancreas is derived solely from the ventral pancreatic bud?

   - **D.** Tail — Wrong. Dorsal bud.
   - **E.** Uncinate process **(Correct)** — Correct. The ventral bud forms the uncinate process; both buds contribute to the head and main duct.
   - Page: 367

3. A 35-year-old woman has recurrent pancreatitis without gallstones or alcohol use. MRCP shows the dorsal pancreatic duct draining most of the gland through the minor papilla. What is the embryologic cause?
